What these numbers assume

  • The reward is 0.40% of each purchase paid with money that arrived through eLacag. It comes out of the 1.5% eLacag charges transfer companies.
  • Cash sales, and money that did not arrive through eLacag, do not earn a reward.
  • The remittance share and eLacag use are your estimates. Change them above to match your store.
  • A month is counted as 30 trading days. All figures are in US dollars.
  • eLacag is in development. Phase I, the smartphone wallet on BPC SmartVista, is nearing completion.
  • Final reward terms are set in the merchant agreement.
